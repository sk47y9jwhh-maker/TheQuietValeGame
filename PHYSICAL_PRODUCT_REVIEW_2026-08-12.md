# The Quiet Vale — Physical Product Review

**Review date:** 12 August 2026
**Purpose:** consolidate all physical-product work, expose conflicting specifications, and provide a task register from the present files to a manufacturable final game.

**Superseding status note — 21 August 2026:** The Target Card decision has since moved to a 30-card, 63 × 88 mm poker-size portrait deck. Cards 25–30 are Open cards with Any for every preference and no arrow; they preserve the originating effect's eligible pool and hand the final choice to the player. The older 24-card references below remain historical review text until the workbook and physical production files are reconciled under TC-006.

## Executive conclusion

The project has a mature visual direction and several genuinely locked source families, but it is **not yet one production-locked product**. The most advanced areas are the modular boards, box artwork, component data, tile template geometry, Encounter master templates, and rules content. The least complete areas are the Target Card product, Steward identity/marker system, full Encounter Card population, commissioned Tile artwork, the Steward's Ledger as a physical object, final token engineering, and cross-component manufacturing reconciliation.

The immediate goal should be a **Physical Product Freeze**, not more isolated artwork. Freeze one bill of materials, one component form factor per item, one rules version, and one approved visual direction. Only then should printer-specific derivatives and proofs be commissioned.

## 1. Proposed final product architecture for sign-off

This is the cleanest product definition supported by the current rules and source files. Treat it as the decision baseline; change it only through an explicit component decision log.

### Shared play surfaces

- **1 map board:** 698.5 × 568.5 mm, six-fold, with exact 60 mm point-to-point hex geometry.
- **1 Encounter board:** 568.5 × 146 mm, centre bi-fold.
- **1 Logistics board:** 568.5 × 146 mm, centre bi-fold, incorporating Target Deck/Encounter/discard areas, the six Warehouses, token holding, and Round/Season tracking.
- **1 Core Tiles supply board:** 284.25 × 232.83 mm, flat.
- **Decision:** formally retire the older single 34.5 × 24.5 in production board, A1 studies, standalone Target Cards board, individual Warehouse modules, and separate Steward board as production candidates if the modular four-board strategy is confirmed.

### Cards and role materials

- **84 large Encounter Cards:** 27 Boons, 27 Burdens, 25 Arrivals and 5 Golden Boons.
- **24 Target Cards:** one continuous face-down deck; cards 13–24 repeat the twelve preference profiles with opposite arrows.
- **6 Steward role products:** one for Vanguard, Knight, Sentinel, Ranger, Warden and Quartermaster. Current work supports either a concise identity card plus shared rules aids, or a 12-page A6 Tile Board booklet per Steward; select one architecture.
- **1 shared quick-reference aid** if Steward booklets are not intended to be held and consulted throughout play.

### Tiles

- **93 physical tile components / 109 physical hexes at stock quantity.**
- Core: 62 physical components, duplex Basic/Upgraded.
- Special: 26 physical components, numbered backs 1–26; the two detached Stables copies account for extra hex faces.
- Golden: 5 one-sided components with blank backs.
- All tile faces: exact 60.0000 × 51.9615 mm flat-top hex geometry.

### Markers and tracking pieces

- **6 Steward markers, one role-specific marker per Steward.** The current order note's eight generic colour-choice pawns conflicts with the six-role game and should not be the final specification.
- **Resource stock:** enough pieces to represent up to 15 of each of six resource types simultaneously. The proposed 90 shared white discs can meet the arithmetic but not the usability/accessibility standard unless the Warehouses remain perfectly separated; six distinguishable resource sets are recommended.
- **Strain:** minimum proven count required by stress testing plus contingency; the present proposal is 25 black discs.
- **Supported:** separate, instantly recognisable positive-state markers.
- **Arrival timers:** three per simultaneously active Arrival at the tested maximum.
- **1 Round marker.**
- **Decision:** do not order a single undifferentiated pool of 130 white discs until a physical state-count and table-readability test proves the allocation.

### Printed books and persistent record

- **Main rulebook:** current v6.4 source is 18 US-Letter pages; manufacturing notes elsewhere assume 20 A4 pages. Choose the actual finished trim and binding, then reflow to a booklet page count divisible by four.
- **Rules Reference:** v6.3 exists and must be reconciled to v6.4 Target/Encounter terminology before manufacture.
- **Steward materials / Player Aid:** select the six 12-page A6 Tile Board booklets or a smaller role-card plus shared-aid architecture; do not manufacture both by accident.
- **Steward's Ledger:** supply a physical write-on achievement artifact derived from the locked workbook sheet. Its format is currently undefined.
- **Quickstart:** no current final production file was found; either create it or remove it from the BOM and teach from the main rulebook/booklets.

## Implemented player experience

- Illustrated setup, unique Steward selection, optional Ledger/Golden choices and contextual phase guidance. Starting positions use an exact legal-coordinate list, explicit confirmation, a fitted map and keyboard/zoom controls. A player/phase handoff focuses relevant decisions without interrupting routine resource changes.
- Shared-device private hand gates unmount hidden cards. Reveal resets on owner/season changes and undo across owners. Living Table now offers explicit top/middle/bottom choices, readable current-season summaries, full approved card artwork and a final review. Pocket retains its separate staged presentation. Manual hiding preserves the draft.
- Golden Tile setup uses an exact legal-coordinate list, fitted/zoomable map, Steward start markers, keyboard browsing and explicit zero-cost confirmation. The Golden Scroll collects each owner’s choices behind a private hand gate, reviews only counts and resolves once. The Golden Bell distinguishes its free three-token placement from the Arrival’s later completion cost. Both have isolated comparison cases. Compact required choices use the full page width and an expandable resolution order.
- Golden Signet planning separates tile selection, destinations and shared review. The engine validates spaces and whole footprints, including swaps into vacated spaces. The map provides visible legal markers, coordinate choices, cardinal orientations, separate detached-space controls, zoom and keyboard navigation. Final review previews the complete settlement, moved Stewards and support changes on selected or neighbouring tiles. Drafts never change the saved board; Apply commits once.
- The Golden Vial has shared ready, prepared, used and exhausted-supply states. Preparation opens an eligible Core Travel tile and focuses its review, with normal costs and legal placement retained. The bonus expires at round end. The Golden-Eyed Traveller shows pending/active bonus turns, order and the next handoff, with no premature encounter, timer or once-per-round refresh. Solo bonus handoffs also move focus. Both have isolated comparison cases.
- Ranger’s power opens a map and coordinate choice, with visible legal targets, tile inspection and explicit confirmation. Its active reach and end-turn expiry remain visible. Warden’s prevention offer explains the spent seasonal use and retained Burden, then gives named tile/relief buttons, direct switching and an engine-derived preview. Stale Ranger choices are validated in the engine, including Overstrained and wrong-player targets.
- Sentinel, Vanguard and Knight now move directly from preparation to eligible upgrade/placement reviews, with honest 0-action labels, resource payments, preparation counts and retained-turn/Season timing. Pocket keeps the matching action open and has a shortcut that restores the correct category after reload. Unavailable basic Core upgrades explain their specific reach, Strain, action and resource blockers. Upgrade previews include real movement and conditional Supported.
- Prepared Steward modifiers are now restricted to their owner. The prior engine allowed a different Steward to consume them after handoff. Ordinary Boons remain shared. This enforces the existing printed power without changing saved-game format, costs or Season expiry. Encounter reference cards label their owner.
- Each committed decision now creates one Undo step under React StrictMode. Game commits and history updates run outside replayable React state updaters. This was found by new Ranger regressions and verified in browser Undo/Redo across both Ranger and Warden preparation/confirmation boundaries.
- Searchable tile catalogue with category/supply filters, retained selection, empty-result recovery and keyboard browsing. The actual engine previews placement, upgrade and activation costs, resources, movement, Strain/support and required follow-up choices without committing or saving. Production at capacity warns truthfully while preserving legal actions.
- The recommended table uses a full-width view through 900 px. Switching actions/board/encounters retains drafts. A completed board selection returns to actions; detached placements remain on the board until both spaces are chosen. Repeated selection works. Wider desktop keeps the simultaneous board/dock.
- Board fit/zoom, touch/long-press inspection and deliberate keyboard controls: one Tab entry, arrows, Home/End, Ctrl/⌘ Home/End, Enter/Space to select, and I to inspect. Navigate offers coordinate lookup. Inspector Escape/close restores HTML or SVG focus and keeps the current game.
- Round-end previews explain Arrival expiry/timers, Boons leaving play, upcoming seasons, renewed Steward powers and the next seasonal Burden text. They read the current public state without changing it.
- Arrival and Burden reviews show action and resource balances before/after payment, shortages and actual outcomes. A zero-action passive still retains its resource cost. Nothing is spent until confirmation, and Cancel preserves the game. Flexible Food/Goods payment uses one keyboard/touch selector while keeping real engine options and other passives. Pending reviews resume after reload. Unconfirmed option selections reset on reload or Undo into the review and must be checked again.
- A capped-history queue collision is fixed for newly queued and chained Overstrain effects. IDs stay distinct from queued effects and the just-resolved head even within one millisecond. Printed rules and saved-game format are unchanged.
- Clear required-choice resolution. Fixed inherited zero-action free-power navigation and disappearing partial Quartermaster exchange controls. Short windows use document scrolling; view changes target the visible heading instead of hidden nested content or a tall panel's bottom.
- Full end-game review with raw engine score, exact equation, penalty details, final objectives, final board and Ledger recording. It recognises a previous campaign record even when an older board flag differs, and distinguishes the immutable recorded score from the current board's score.
- Reset, Ledger reset and starting a new settlement use a shared in-page confirmation with safe initial focus, keyboard containment, Escape, background isolation and focus return. Cancelling preserves drafts. Board reset keeps campaign history and returns focus to setup. Ledger reset keeps the board and recent game steps, while clearing obsolete recording markers so a retained completed game can be recorded again. Ledger → Settlements presents actual saved scores/dates/Stewards/Vows/entries, newest first, with pagination and honest older-record fallbacks. It does not offer past-board restoration because boards are not stored in campaign history.

- Local saving reports quota, access and removal failures visibly, without blocking the current in-memory game. Retry saves the latest confirmed position after further actions, Undo or reset; unconfirmed hand/board drafts remain in the open tab. A recovery message distinguishes confirmed progress from drafts. Unreadable saved data is preserved unchanged and disables writes for that session. The Ledger is saved before the board's recorded marker; a later load reconciles a stale marker after a partial Ledger clear.

Living Table now has a collapsed **Plan this round** panel above the action controls. Expand it to see Arrival deadlines, the current Steward's objective and any Overstrained tiles. Review Arrival opens and focuses that exact card without changing the game. Interact retains the existing completion rules and costs. Required effects and cost choices take priority over this panel.

In a pay-or-Strain or pay-or-timer effect, the new payment buttons replace the suggested outcome before confirmation. Restore suggested Strain/timer loss returns to the same suggestion without drawing another Target Card. Clear choices supports mixed outcomes through the existing controls. The preview and Apply Effect remain separate. Effects with no eligible outcomes explain why there is nothing to choose, including Help Stands when only Strain removal applies.

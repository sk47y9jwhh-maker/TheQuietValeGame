# Main Rulebook v7.0 (Complete Blind-Playtest Redesign — Pages 2–35)

Versioned redesign built from `design/rulebook-v6_9` (reusing its shared component artwork in `design/rulebook-v6_9/assets`). Locked v6.6 archives and v6.9 sources remain untouched.

## Summary of Changes (Pages 2–35)
- **Page 2 (Welcome & How You Win):** Expanded goal and full Add/Subtract shared score box upfront.
- **Page 3 (Contents):** Removed "Using this rulebook" / "New to the Vale?" / "Coming back?" filler for a clean Contents page.
- **Page 4 (How a game flows):** Rebuilt overview with 3 Seasons, 3-step round cadence (rotating **Player 1 marker** and **4/3/2 Actions**), and 4 plain-English core concepts introducing **Strain & Overstrained** early.
- **Pages 5–7 (Unified `1 · Components & Setup`):** Paired Components as the visual key to the facing setup layout, added the **Player 1 marker**, added a Steward page pointer, and consolidated **Actions/turn**, **Starting Warehouse (`15/10/8/0/0`)**, and **Encounter splits (`5/3/4 · 8/8/8 · 12/12/12 · 16/16/16 · 26/14/20`)** into one 1–5p table.
- **Page 8 (Seeding):** Cut `"In a normal game"`, explained *why* seeding matters (Season I/II/III rows), and replaced `"Bring it forward"` with `"Seed earlier (Top)"` vs `"Seed later (Bottom or hold in hand)"`.
- **Page 9 (How a round works):** Rotating **Player 1 marker**, 4/3/2 Actions, plain-English `"Finish one effect before moving on"` (replacing `"Pending effects"` jargon), and removed distracting Golden box.
- **Pages 10–11 (Your first round):** Uses **Player 1 marker** and replaces the intimidating 7-column Warehouse table with a simple plain-English recap.
- **Page 12 (The four Actions):** Plain-English rewrites of repeating actions, **Moving your Steward & Reach**, **Upgrade**, and **Interact / Burdens**, with an inline Overstrained reminder.
- **Page 13 (Linked Production):** Simplified wording and clear **X token** instructions (marks Linked Production spent for the round while single-tile activation stays legal).
- **Page 14 (Reading a Tile):** Cut awkward symbol-reading phrasing and rewrote once-per-round / once-per-Season refresh rules in plain English.
- **Page 15 (Placing Tiles / Cooldowns):** Explicitly added **X token** placement & round-end removal for Workshops / Makers Conclave and Market Stalls / The Seldes.
- **Page 16 (Reach, Rivers & Bridges):** Plain-English reachability definition, deleted out-of-place flavor text, and prominently highlighted **Overstrained tiles breaking the connected network**.
- **Page 17 (Special Tiles):** Corrected rule so placing an unlocked Special Tile costs **0 Actions and 0 resources** (after spending 1 Interact Action to complete the Arrival), and trimmed redundant placement text.
- **Pages 18–19 (Encounter Cards):** Removed duplication with Page 8; streamlined card reading and Boons/Burdens in play.
- **Page 20 (Arrivals):** Added prominent callout for **failed Arrival set-aside (−10 points) AND immediate 1 Strain**.
- **Page 21 (Strain & Supported):** Clear 1–2 vs 3 (Overstrained + cascade) thresholds on Page 21 and plain-English **Passive vs Single-Use Supported** bronze shield token rules.
- **Page 31 (Twin Settlements):** Fixed `"shaded gold"` mismatch on East Vale map description.
- **Pages 32–35 (Quick Reference, Questions & Glossary):** Aligned Quick Reference & Questions with v7.0 (0-Action Special Tiles, Player 1 marker, Passive Supported, Failed Arrival Strain) and **integrated page references (`p. X`) directly into every Glossary definition** (replacing the separate index list).

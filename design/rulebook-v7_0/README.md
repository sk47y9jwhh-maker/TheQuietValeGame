# Main Rulebook v7.0 (Blind-Playtest Redesign — Pages 2–12)

Versioned redesign built from `design/rulebook-v6_9` (reusing its shared component artwork in `design/rulebook-v6_9/assets`). Locked v6.6 archives remain untouched.

## Changes in v7.0 (from Blind-Playtest Feedback)
- **Page 2 (Welcome):** Expanded "How you play & how you win" with a full Add/Subtract shared score breakdown upfront.
- **Page 3 (Contents):** Removed "Using this rulebook" / "New to the Vale?" / "Coming back?" boxes for a clean, direct Contents page.
- **Page 4 (How a game flows):** Reworked overview into 3 Seasons, 3-step round cadence (with rotating Player 1 marker and 4/3/2 Actions), and 4 plain-English core concepts including early context for **Strain & Overstrained**.
- **Pages 5–7 (Unified `1 · Components & Setup`):** Paired Components as the visual key to the facing setup layout, added the **Player 1 marker**, and consolidated Actions/Turn, Starting Warehouse (`15/10/8/0/0`), and Encounter splits (`5/3/4 · 8/8/8 · 12/12/12 · 16/16/16 · 26/14/20`) into one unified 1–5 player Setup table.
- **Page 8 (Seeding):** Removed `"In a normal game"`, explained *why* seeding matters (Season I/II/III rows), and replaced `"Bring it forward"` with `"Seed earlier (Top)"` vs `"Seed later (Bottom or hold in hand)"`.
- **Page 9 (How a round works):** Added rotating Player 1 marker, 4/3/2 Action counts, replaced `"Pending effects"` jargon with `"Finish one effect before moving on"`, and removed the distracting Golden content callout.
- **Pages 10–11 (Your first round):** Updated to use the Player 1 marker and replaced the dense 7-column Warehouse table on Page 11 with a concise plain-English recap.
- **Page 12 (The four Actions):** Rewrote repeating actions, Steward movement & Reach (replacing `"Your meeple marks where you reach from"`), Upgrade, and Interact/Burden rules in plain English, with an inline Overstrained reminder.
